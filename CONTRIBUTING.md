on request.

For that dual-licensing to remain possible, the project maintainer must hold the
rights to relicense all of the code. If the project accepted contributions under
the AGPL only, the maintainer could no longer offer a commercial license without
each contributor's permission.

Therefore, **by submitting a contribution (pull/merge request, patch, etc.) you
agree that you grant the maintainer a perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable license
to use, modify, relicense and sublicense your contribution — including under the
project's commercial license — while you retain the copyright to your own work.**

If you are contributing on behalf of an employer, make sure you have the right to
agree to the above.

If you are not comfortable with these terms, please open an issue to discuss
before contributing rather than sending code.
